## Mathematical checks and resolved conflicts

- All central proofs were reconstructed independently, including conditional-information shift and its strict-concavity equality case, boundary completions, compatible safety corridor and stopping-time projection. Numerical identities are never presented as proofs.
- Coarse sharpness is explicitly sharp for the supplied partition's model; the coarse interval is a relaxation if finer observed margins are known.
- The theorem is per fixed partition. A data-adaptive best-of-four certificate is not justified by four unadjusted per-partition regions.
- Equal absolute rho and equal excess rho-tau answer different questions. The exact effective-budget identity is made explicit.
- Roadmap entropy-envelope explanation incorrectly suggests the raw coordinate-upper entropy sum is not a valid upper bound when unattainable. Coordinate monotonicity makes it valid but loose. The paper and Supplement give the correct proof and treat clipping as tightening, without altering source code.
- Historical campaign counts of 240 oracle cases and 48 theorem cases are stale: current records use14 laws (280 solver cases,56 common identity cases). Current records control manuscript numbers.
- Campaign calls a 100/100 power limit one-sided in one sentence; stored/reporting formulas use two-sided 95% limits (.963783307...), which the article uses. Coverage/false-event limits are separately one-sided.
- `premature_certifications` in power.py counts only certificates in COMPATIBLE_NOT_CERTIFIABLE cells, not every incompatible model. The article states this scope and does not equate the zero counter with zero certificates under a false sensitivity assumption.
- Coverage code can accumulate consumer failure entries across looks. In the included current artifacts, construction and consumer totals agree and nonzero consumer counts come solely from one empty-region event per affected stream; no multiple-look endpoint violation is used as a binomial stream count. The statement is limited to these verified artifacts.
- Archived pytest_final.log has961 passing tests and92.22% coverage, whereas the campaign text later reports92.01%. This manuscript does not report a precise coverage percentage. Historical tests were read, not rerun.
