## Three adversarial review perspectives

These are three analytical reviews of the revised manuscript by the editing agent, not claims of independent human reviewers or external peer review.

1. **Mathematical theory.** Challenged whether timing gain merely changes the assumption, whether coarse sharpness survives known finer margins, and whether boundary/saturation cases invalidate strictness. The fixed common total budget, equal-excess identity, supplied-margin definition of sharpness and explicit boundary clauses resolve these issues. The contribution is specialized profile and frontier geometry; related general divergence and identification results are attributed. No universal novelty claim remains.
2. **Sequential inference and computation.** Challenged observable versus latent stability, optional stopping, simultaneous partition selection, empty regions, relaxation tightness, approximate witnesses and numerical signs. The theorem includes exact-region containment and conservative projection hypotheses; category/time/partition multiplicity and all exits are distinguished. The inherited claim that every accepted confidence sign is Arb-certified was contradicted by `_float_side` and corrected. A uniform filter derivation remains unavailable; tests cannot settle that objection. Historical run source identity also remains unverified.
3. **Experimental validity and practical relevance.** Challenged pilot-informed selection, shared streams, unfavorable controls, optimizer nonresolution, invalid sensitivity budgets, old timing exports and transport diagnostics. The revision separates exploratory design from the 100-stream estimates, reports all final cells and current timings, preserves the zero incompatibility detection and negative controls, and explains secondary-grid errors without treating them as calibrated error probabilities. Synthetic applicability and hardware limits remain explicit.

Re-reading the corrected text did not reveal a justified reason to weaken the population theorems or discard the finite-sample evidence. The missing numerical contract remains unresolved; author approval was subsequently confirmed by the user.
