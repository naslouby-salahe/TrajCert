# Reviewer Audit

## Likely objections

| Reviewer concern | Current evidence | Required framing or work |
|---|---|---|
| Is the method novel? | Roadmap §2.4 compares endpoint bounds, callback adjustment and identification, Little and Shardell–El-Kamary pattern-mixture/coarsening methods, Kline–Santos bounded-selection sensitivity, Cro et al. information anchoring, anytime inference, and the in-project legacy comparator using primary sources. The targeted search did not establish an equivalent `I(L;J*)` constraint, which is not proof of absence. | Treat informative coarsening as a close conceptual neighbor. Describe only the model-specific sharp interval, coarsening/timing results, and conservative projection; make no priority claim and revisit equivalence at manuscript review. |
| Is `rho` plausible? | No calibration or elicitation study is present. | Treat `rho` as a sensitivity axis. Explain how a future application could elicit it; do not call the chosen grid realistic. |
| Does finer timing help in practice? | The theorem identifies conditions; simulation can show behavior under declared laws. | Separate theorem from synthetic utility and report nulls and effect heterogeneity. No operational benefit claim follows from the current evidence. |
| Does HITL-IoT validate the method externally? | The CSV is external to the repo; annotation provenance is not verified. Reviewed status is strongly associated with attack errors. | Label the route diagnostic-only and describe the reviewed-cohort selection. Do not generalize to all flows, annotators, or devices. |
| Are the coverage simulations one global guarantee? | Twelve per-condition 95% intervals are used; no joint campaign-wide coverage level is established. | Present per-condition results as diagnostics. Do not interpret “all pass” as familywise 95% evidence. Predefine multiplicity and power if a joint empirical claim is needed. |
| Are events independent or stationary? | Generated streams are IID by construction; the raw external file spans seven days and is not independent stream replication. | Limit simulation claims to the generating law. Add dependence and drift stress cases only if those settings are part of the intended claim. |
| Is utility inference adequately powered? | The independent unit is a synthetic stream; methods share 6,000 streams per law/rho condition, and 54 tests receive Holm adjustment. A design-only exact integration evaluates the complete gate at a 0.07 planning alternative fixed before campaign execution and informed by pre-campaign precision/power analysis, against the unchanged 0.05 observed-effect cutoff; modeled complete-gate power is 83.3959%. | Treat 0.07 only as a prospective methodological sensitivity, not an empirically validated domain threshold or minimum worthwhile effect. It does not establish effectiveness. A result near 0.05 can satisfy the observed-effect filter without having target power. Do not interpret a null outside the planning scenario as evidence against the mechanism. |
| Are reported outputs reproducible? | Planning and verified-output reporting are wired; inherited results directories exist without a fresh public run in this audit. | Bind every reported value to current config, source checksum, code revision, and artifact provenance. Do not treat stale outputs as current evidence. |

## Decision

The paper is viable as a theory and methods paper if it keeps its claims within the theorem and synthetic experiment assumptions. A claim of validated HITL-IoT performance or practical deployment value is not supported. Roadmap §2.4 now gives the primary-source comparator discussion; manuscript preparation still needs a final citation and equivalence review, the external-data discrepancy and selection disclosure, the pointwise scope of empirical confidence intervals, and clear labeling of synthetic versus diagnostic evidence.
